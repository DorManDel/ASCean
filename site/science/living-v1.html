Verified source statements, illustrative models

NOAA Ocean Service explains increasing pressure with vertical water depth. We use P = 101325 + ρ × 9.80665 × h, converting h from cm to m. Uniform density and water properties are fixture assumptions; no pressure tolerance or survival response is modeled.

Mearns et al., Diverse prey capture strategies in teleost larvae (2025), DOI 10.7554/eLife.98347, compares larval freshwater teleost prey-capture strategies. Species differ; a generic vision cone is not an experimentally validated model of those species. Article: CC BY, attribution required. No figures, data or coefficients were imported.

The simplified FOV/occlusion filter, acceleration and turning limits, hunger-as-gut-capacity-deficit, ingestion range/rate, current and habitat presets are all illustrative. Two synthetic species presets are not real named species, and habitat compatibility is a software fixture constraint, not a salinity-tolerance claim. Life stage is unspecified; no inheritance or development is active.

Calibration proposals

Movement: fit turn rate, acceleration and water-relative speed to species/life-stage trajectories measured with current removed. Vision: compare detection probability against angle, range, contrast and occlusion under known illumination. Ingestion: measure eligible-resource capture range, handling time and consumed wet/dry mass consistently. Food ledger uses only abstract material grams transferred to gut; energy, digestion and waste require separate reviewed models.
