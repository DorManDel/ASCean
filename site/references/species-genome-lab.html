What this prototype does—and does not—model

Diploid loci, random segregation and rare bounded mutation. Editing sliders changes the heritable genotype, not merely appearance. Pigmentation and morphology are inherited. This does not implement linked chromosomes, crossing-over, mating eligibility, selection pressure or real molecular genetics yet. Body parts remain species constrained and offspring are not automatically introduced into a running world.

Matched Control vs Treatment

Both groups start with the same fish, injury and regeneration trait. The only experimental variable is damage to the fin's structural base. Toy coefficients are illustrative, not biologically calibrated.
