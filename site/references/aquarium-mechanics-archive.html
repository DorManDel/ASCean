Pressure follows the hydrostatic approximation P = P₀ + ρgz. Light is an illustrative attenuation curve; real spectra depend on wavelength, water clarity, season and location.


What changes with depth?
  • Pressure increases with water depth.
  • Light decreases; color changes with the wavelengths that remain.
  • Temperature, oxygen and currents vary with region and time.
  • Fish may choose depths based on food, shelter, temperature and species biology.


Depth ≠ distance into the screen
  • Y: vertical height in our world; hydrostatic depth comes from water surface minus Y.
  • Z: distance into the side-view screen — it is not water depth.
  • The original World is 100m × 50m × 30m (X × Y × Z). This demo explores deeper habitats separately.

The flow we'll model
Foodcomposition, availability→Digestgut compartments, time→Absorbnutrients to reserves→Allocatemaintenance / growth / reproduction
  • Unassimilated material exits as feces.
  • Nitrogenous wastes, including ammonia in many fish, can enter water through gills.
  • Digestion and reproductive strategies depend on species and life stage.
  • Not every food item is edible or safe: ingestion is distinct from assimilation.


Research gates before coding metabolism
  • Diet and feeding strategy by species and developmental stage.
  • Food energy/nutrient composition with source and units.
  • Digestive anatomy (zebrafish are stomachless).
  • Assimilation, respiration and excretion coefficients from literature.
  • Mass/energy balance and waste transfer into the environment.
