ASCII does not define anatomy

The anatomical template is independent of whether it is painted with 50 glyphs, 2,000 glyphs, geometric paths or a cached bitmap. Glyph cells can carry a region ID, but no organ should be represented only by a display character.

In this demo the vector paths, organ landmarks and glyphs share a local coordinate system. Anatomical shapes and injury responses are illustrative—not measured species anatomy.

What should be true in ASCean?

• Anatomy must exist even if the fish is not rendered.
• Character cells are a visual sampling of body regions.
• Removing skin reveals tissue; deeper injury can affect organ-state records.
• Juveniles inherit their species anatomy blueprint, not a fixed ASCII string.
• The actual coral solver remains separate from its neon/ASCII appearance.
